01 / THE EXPERIENCE

What is Nocturnia?

A different kind of MUN experience.

Nocturnia MUN is an online night conference built around one idea: diplomacy does not clock out.

From the first motion to the final crisis update, Nocturnia is designed to keep the room moving. Expect focused debate, competitive committees, late-night strategy and an atmosphere that feels different from the usual conference.

And because a good night deserves a reward, outstanding participants will have the chance to compete for cash prizes.
